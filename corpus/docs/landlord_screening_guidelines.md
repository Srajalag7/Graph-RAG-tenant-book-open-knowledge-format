# openigloo Landlord Screening Guidelines

<!-- doc_id: landlord_screening_guidelines | issuer: openigloo | source_url: internal | effective_from: 2025-03-01 | effective_to: None | supersedes: None | superseded_by: None | visibility: landlord,staff | version: 1 -->

## Who this document is for

This document is provided to landlords and property managers who list on openigloo, and to openigloo staff. It is not shown to renters. It describes the screening practices openigloo requires of landlords on the platform. Renters who ask why an application was declined are directed to ask the landlord directly and are informed of their right to a copy of any consumer report used.

## Income requirements

Landlords on openigloo may require that a household's income be no more than 40 times the monthly rent, or an equivalent ratio. Where any part of the rent will be paid by a voucher or subsidy, the income requirement may be applied only to the portion of rent the household pays. A landlord may not apply the full-rent income requirement to a voucher holder. Requiring a guarantor from a voucher holder solely because of the voucher is not permitted.

## Credit and background checks

Landlords may collect no more than $20 for an application, background or credit check, and must accept a report the applicant provides that is dated within the last 30 days. Landlords may not decline an applicant solely because of a prior housing court case. openigloo removes listings from landlords found to be using tenant blacklists.

## Declining an applicant

A landlord who declines an applicant must tell the applicant in writing within 5 business days and, if a consumer report was used, must give the applicant the name of the reporting agency. openigloo does not require landlords to state a reason, but a landlord may not state a reason that is unlawful, including source of income, family status, national origin, or any other protected class.

## What openigloo does with a decline

openigloo records the decline in the internal listing record. Three or more declines of voucher holders on the same listing within 60 days trigger a review by the openigloo trust team, which may contact the landlord and may remove the listing.
