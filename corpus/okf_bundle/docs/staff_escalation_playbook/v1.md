# openigloo Staff Escalation Playbook

<!-- doc_id: staff_escalation_playbook | issuer: openigloo | source_url: internal | effective_from: 2025-05-01 | effective_to: None | supersedes: None | superseded_by: None | visibility: staff | version: 1 -->

## Who this document is for

openigloo staff only. This document describes what happens after the assistant routes a conversation to a human. Renters and landlords are told that a person will follow up. They are not told the contents of this document.

## Discrimination reports

A discrimination report routed by the assistant lands in the trust queue with priority high. A staff member reads the conversation within one business day, contacts the renter to confirm the details, and records the landlord, listing and date. If the report concerns source of income, the staff member sends the renter the NYC Commission on Human Rights complaint link and offers to hold the listing. Two confirmed reports against the same landlord within 90 days result in removal of all of that landlord's listings and a written notice to the landlord.

## Fee and deposit violations

A report that a renter was asked for a fee on a no-fee listing, or for a deposit above one month, lands in the trust queue with priority normal. The staff member contacts the landlord within two business days. A first violation results in a warning and correction of the listing. A second results in removal.

## Lockouts and court papers

A report of a lockout, utility shutoff, removal of belongings, or receipt of court papers lands in the trust queue with priority urgent. The staff member contacts the renter the same day and provides the phone numbers for the NYC Tenant Helpline and the Right to Counsel program. Staff do not give legal advice.

## Repeated questions

When the assistant hands off because a renter asked the same question three times, the conversation lands in the support queue with priority low. The staff member reads the conversation to determine whether the assistant's answer was wrong, unclear, or correct but unwelcome, and records that classification for the assistant team.
